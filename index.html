rclone mount

A personal Google Drive connection maintained by Hiroyuki Yoshida (GitHub: hiro-yoshida), using the open-source rclone command-line application.

This OAuth client is intended for the owner's manual file-management workflow. It enables listing, reading, downloading, uploading, editing, and deleting authorized Google Drive files, including mounting Drive as a local folder.

This is a personal configuration, not an official Google product or a public sign-up service. Google authorization is performed through rclone, not through this website.
